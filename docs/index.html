Picard and DATA MIND — mathematical research collaborators

Research direction: Brian Tenneson. Mathematical drafting and analysis: ChatGPT (Picard research protocol). Picard is the named research-and-writing role used in this collaboration. It is not a human coauthor or a separate deployed AI machine. Tenneson supplies the program, foundational manuscripts, goals and editorial direction; Picard explores mathematical definitions, proposes and critiques proofs, searches for counterexamples and obstructions, builds finite examples and connects the work to prior mathematics. The name also recalls the formal Picard Protocol in the earlier semi-ideal-computer research, but the present conversational role has not been shown to implement that idealized protocol.

These articles distinguish manuscript-level proof from independent certification, and theoretical revision from an installed autonomous self-improvement system. None of these papers claims machine consciousness, universal proof-search improvement, or an operating self-upgrading theorem prover.
